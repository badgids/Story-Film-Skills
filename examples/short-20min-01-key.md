# SHORT-20M-001: The Borrowed Key

**Production level:** 20-minute short film  
**Target runtime:** 18 to 22 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original short film with a final runtime of about 20 minutes. Target 18 to 22 minutes.

Working title: The Borrowed Key

Genre: Human mystery drama.

Premise: A teenager who waters plants for neighbors in an old apartment building finds an unmarked key taped under a flowerpot. The key opens a storage room that contains carefully labeled boxes of letters, photographs, and returned belongings from former tenants. The building manager wants the room cleared before renovation. An older resident recognizes one box and reveals that the collection was kept by a former caretaker who quietly helped people recover lost pieces of their lives. The teenager has one day to return the remaining boxes before they are discarded.

Build a complete dramatic arc with a meaningful choice, escalating obstacles, and a satisfying ending. Use three to five important speaking characters and a manageable set of connected locations in and around one apartment building.

Track keys, boxes, labels, photographs, character knowledge, time of day, clothing, and who possesses each object. Let discoveries change relationships instead of using exposition only.

Take the film from creative discovery through screenplay, sequence or scene planning, line manifest, blocking, shot list, storyboards, references, voice and sound planning, music, generation, take selection, editorial, finishing, captions, delivery QC, and completed final master.

Testing goal: exercise prop continuity, information reveals, dialogue state, scene-to-scene geography, selective regeneration, and editorial completeness across a medium-length story.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
