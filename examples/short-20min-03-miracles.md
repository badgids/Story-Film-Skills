# SHORT-20M-003: The Committee for Small Miracles

**Production level:** 20-minute short film  
**Target runtime:** 18 to 22 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original short film with a final runtime of about 20 minutes. Target 18 to 22 minutes.

Working title: The Committee for Small Miracles

Genre: Warm ensemble comedy with light fantasy.

Premise: In a plain municipal office, a secret committee reviews requests for tiny impossible favors: finding a lost recipe, making a broken music box play once more, giving someone the courage to make a phone call, or letting a garden bloom one day early. A new clerk thinks the work is absurd until one request arrives from someone connected to the clerk's own past. The committee must decide whether helping would break its strict rules.

Use four to six speaking characters with distinct voices and motivations. Keep the fantasy modest and visually specific. The comedy should come from personalities, rules, and consequences rather than cruelty.

Create clear rules for what the committee can and cannot do. Track each request, vote, consequence, prop, character relationship, and rule exception. The ending must resolve the central emotional choice while leaving the world larger than the story.

Take the project through story development, screenplay, cast and performance design, blocking, production documents, shot planning, visual references, dialogue and voice production, fantasy-effect briefs, music/SFX, generation, take selection, editorial, finishing, QC, and final master.

Testing goal: exercise ensemble dialogue, character identity, rule-based world continuity, multiple props, comedic timing, effect planning, and medium-length production management.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
