# VIDEO-5M-001: The Last Light in the Workshop

**Production level:** 5-minute video  
**Target runtime:** 4 to 6 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original cinematic video with a final runtime of about 5 minutes. Target 4 to 6 minutes.

Working title: The Last Light in the Workshop

Premise: On the final evening before a small repair shop closes, a careful teenage apprentice helps an older clock repairer finish one last broken pocket watch. The watch belongs to a customer who needs it for an important family memory. The repair becomes a quiet lesson about patience, craft, and what people choose to pass on.

Create an emotionally clear beginning, middle, and ending. Keep the story small enough for a 5-minute production. Use two main speaking characters, one workshop location, and a small number of important props. Make the visual storytelling strong enough that the audience can understand important emotions even when nobody speaks.

Use original characters, original dialogue, original music direction, and original visual designs. Do not imitate a protected film, franchise, living artist, or existing score.

Take the project from story development through screenplay, scene and line IDs, character and location references, blocking, shot design, storyboards or previz, voice and sound planning, music and SFX planning, generation briefs, ComfyUI-ready work, take selection, editorial, finishing, delivery QC, and a completed final master. Use the resource-safe ComfyUI handoff when the local LLM and generation models cannot safely share memory.

Keep continuity simple but explicit. Track the watch, tools, hand positions, clothing, workbench layout, and the changing light outside the shop. Validate every production stage before advancing the Todo.

Testing goal: prove that Story-Film Skills can complete a compact narrative video end to end without losing dialogue, prop continuity, shot intent, or final delivery state.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
