## Sheet types

Story-Film supports character, location, and prop reference-sheet plans. View counts are production choices, not universal rules.

## Prop functional views

A prop plan may request a mechanical or functional detail in addition to exterior angles. This is useful when later action depends on a latch, control, mechanism, articulated part, or interface.

## Staged grounding

Complex storyboard grounding may proceed from composition to environment/style to character/prop identity, preserving authority scopes between passes.
