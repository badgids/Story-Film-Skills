## Classification

Measure approved WAV duration before expensive video generation. A line or clip is `fits`, `needs-timing-rebalance`, or `impossible`.

## No silent repair

The preflight never speeds up, truncates, rewrites, reassigns, or silently overlaps dialogue. Impossible timing returns upstream to shot design.
