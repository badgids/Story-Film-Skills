# Idea to Book

1. `story-brief`: include book form and target scale.
2. `story-architecture`.
3. `character-bible`.
4. `world-bible` when recurring setting rules matter.
5. `beat-sheet`: use major story movements, not one beat per chapter.
6. `book-plan`: create complete `CH-###` architecture through the ending.
7. `write-book`: draft one chapter at a time and update chapter state after each.
8. `revise-book`: whole-book pass first, then chapter and prose passes.
9. `continuity-check` and style check.

Done when all planned chapters exist, the ending resolves the central dramatic question, and chapter state matches the manuscript.
