# Story to Screenplay

1. Read the entire source story or the complete relevant range.
2. `adapt-screenplay`: produce an adaptation map and screen decisions.
3. `character-bible` and `world-bible`: reconcile source facts into canon.
4. `scene-outline`: create the screen scene plan.
5. `write-screenplay`.
6. `revise-screenplay`.
7. `continuity-check`. Done when every major source function is preserved, intentionally transformed, or explicitly omitted in the adaptation map.
