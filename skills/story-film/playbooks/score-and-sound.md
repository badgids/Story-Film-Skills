# Score and Sound

1. Read screenplay, director book if present, and edit intent.
2. `score-plan`: create music cues and leitmotif rules.
3. `sound-design`: create ambience, Foley, hard effects, transitions, and silence plan.
4. Use `ace-step-xl` or `minimax-music-3` for music.
5. Use `stable-audio-3` for SFX, ambience, stems, or additional instrumental material.
6. `prompt-qc`. Done when every cue has a dramatic or physical job, timing, and target model.
