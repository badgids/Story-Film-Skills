# Creative Pressure Test

Route the questioning through `decision-tree-interview`.

Focus the interview on failure modes appropriate to the requested medium, including where relevant:

- premise, theme, audience, tone, point of view
- character desire, conflict, stakes, agency, contradiction, transformation
- world rules, causality, chronology, setup/payoff, promise fulfillment
- scene purpose, escalation, pacing, emotional logic
- visual continuity, blocking, coverage, geography, camera motivation
- dialogue, voice identity, music, sound, silence, intelligibility
- generation feasibility, reference quality, model limits, resource limits
- edit structure, delivery formats, trailer spoiler policy, campaign claims
- budget, schedule, human approvals, legal/rights constraints when declared

Do not start production merely because enough questions have been answered to imagine a solution. Finish the active decision frontier and confirm shared understanding first.
