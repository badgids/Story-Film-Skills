# Screenplay Craft Reference

## Screen direction

Write what can be seen or heard. Keep paragraphs short. Use present tense. Do not write camera directions unless the shot itself matters to meaning, staging, or the user's requested directing plan.

## Scene headings

Use Fountain-compatible headings:

```text
INT. GARAGE - NIGHT
EXT. COUNTY ROAD - DAWN
INT./EXT. TRUCK - MOVING - DAY
```

## Action

Describe the smallest set of visible actions needed to stage and understand the scene. Avoid prose-novel interiority that cannot be filmed.

## Dialogue

Character names are uppercase above dialogue. Parentheticals are rare and only clarify delivery that the words and action cannot.

## Adaptation

When adapting prose:

1. identify the dramatic function of the prose passage
2. convert internal information into behavior, dialogue, visual motif, omission, or staging
3. combine repetitive locations and beats where screen time would drag
4. preserve causal logic and character choices
5. record meaningful adaptation changes in the scene manifest

## Page economy

Do not chase a page count by padding. Target runtime is a constraint, not a quota. After a complete draft, estimate runtime by scene and tighten where pacing is wrong.
