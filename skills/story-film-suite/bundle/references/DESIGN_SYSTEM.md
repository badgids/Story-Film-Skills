# Film and Campaign Design System

A visual campaign should have a reusable design grammar before it has dozens of individual graphics.

Use `06_release/artwork/design_system.json`.

Recommended fields:

- visual concept and design principles
- source `REF-###` identities
- palette roles rather than arbitrary hex accumulation
- typography hierarchy and fallback policy
- title treatment
- spacing and grid rules
- recurring shapes, borders, textures, and motifs
- image treatment
- character and location prominence rules
- title-safe and subtitle-safe zones
- logo and watermark rules
- still-image aspect adaptations
- motion behavior for `GFX-###` and `COMP-###`
- accessibility contrast requirements
- forbidden visual shortcuts
- exact text that must remain unchanged

The design system should describe the project's own visual identity. Do not imitate a living artist or copy a protected campaign layout. References can identify craft qualities and production constraints without cloning protected expression.

A campaign asset may vary by platform while remaining visibly part of the same system.
