# VIDEO-5M-002: Where Rain Goes

**Production level:** 5-minute video  
**Target runtime:** 4 to 6 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original educational mini-documentary with a final runtime of about 5 minutes. Target 4 to 6 minutes.

Working title: Where Rain Goes

Subject: Follow one ordinary rainstorm from a city street to storm drains, small streams, a river, evaporation, clouds, and another rainfall. Explain the basic water-cycle and storm-water path in language that a young teenager can understand.

This is a factual project. Research public scientific claims before using them in narration. Keep a claim ledger and source support for statements that are presented as facts. If reliable sources disagree about a detail, do not hide the disagreement.

Use a friendly narrator, clear visual examples, simple diagrams or motion graphics where useful, natural rain and water sound, and restrained original music. Avoid fear-based environmental claims. Do not state a local drainage fact unless the project has evidence for that location.

Take the project from research and treatment through narration script, visual plan, shot/graphic plan, audio plan, generation briefs, ComfyUI-ready assets, motion graphics, edit, captions, final mix, delivery QC, and a completed final master. Produce human-readable Markdown companions for any rich production documents.

Testing goal: prove that Story-Film Skills can combine evidence-backed research, narration, generated visuals, graphics, captions, audio, and final delivery in a short factual video.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
