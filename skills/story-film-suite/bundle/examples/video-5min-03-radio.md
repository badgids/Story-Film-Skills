# VIDEO-5M-003: Midnight Radio

**Production level:** 5-minute video  
**Target runtime:** 4 to 6 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original dialogue-driven cinematic video with a final runtime of about 5 minutes. Target 4 to 6 minutes.

Working title: Midnight Radio

Premise: During a severe storm, two volunteer hosts are finishing the last hour of a tiny community radio shift. The power flickers. A frightened caller reports that an elderly neighbor may be stranded nearby. The hosts must decide whether to stay on air as an information lifeline or leave the station to help in person. They find a way to do both by organizing listeners.

Use two visible main characters, one primary radio-studio location, one caller heard only by voice, and a clear emotional turn. Dialogue must sound natural and must not repeat accidentally. Lip movement, speaker identity, reaction shots, radio timing, and storm sound must stay synchronized.

Use original characters, dialogue, sound design, music direction, station branding, and visual design. Do not imitate a real radio program or copyrighted franchise.

Take the project through screenplay, line manifest, performance blocking, shot design, voice design, dialogue generation, ambience and SFX, ComfyUI-ready video work, take selection, editorial, audio mix, captions, finishing, QC, and final master.

Testing goal: stress dialogue routing, speaker continuity, lip-sync planning, reaction timing, audio layering, and recovery if one generated dialogue or video job fails.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
