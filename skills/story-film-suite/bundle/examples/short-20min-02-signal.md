# SHORT-20M-002: Signal at Mile 18

**Production level:** 20-minute short film  
**Target runtime:** 18 to 22 minutes  
**Purpose:** Example and end-to-end test prompt for Story-Film Skills.

## Prompt

```text
Use Story-Film Skills to create a complete original short film with a final runtime of about 20 minutes. Target 18 to 22 minutes.

Working title: Signal at Mile 18

Genre: Grounded science-fiction road drama.

Premise: A parent and adult child are driving an old utility truck through a remote rural area after years of barely speaking. At the same bend in the road, their radio begins receiving a calm emergency message that appears to be from twenty minutes in the future. The message warns them about an accident ahead. Each new transmission contains a little more information, including details only they should know. They must decide whether the signal is real, a trap, or a chance to change what is about to happen.

Keep the science-fiction element focused and understandable. The emotional story is about trust and unresolved family conflict. Use two main characters, the truck, roadside locations, and one important destination.

Track vehicle damage, fuel, road direction, weather, radio messages, exact message order, character knowledge, injuries, props, and time progression. The future messages must remain logically consistent with earlier scenes.

Take the production through screenplay, scene/sequence planning, dialogue identities, blocking, vehicle geography, shot design, references, voice, radio processing, ambience, music, ComfyUI generation, take selection, continuity validation, edit, finishing, QC, and final master.

Testing goal: stress temporal continuity, repeated-but-changing dialogue, vehicle continuity, night/exterior generation, long-range state tracking, and partial batch recovery.
```

## Test notes

Use the prompt without changes for a full production test. For a faster planning test, use the test modes in [Examples and test prompts](README.md).
