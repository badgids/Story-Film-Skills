## Source rule

Use approved film media as the source.

Do not silently replace an approved film shot with a random newer render.

## Trailers

Plan the trailer purpose and duration.

Select source moments.

Track pickup needs.

Build a trailer timeline.

Master and QC the trailer.

## Social media

Define the campaign and brand voice.

Create cutdowns and reframes.

Keep content lineage from the source film or trailer.

Keep public factual claims tied to evidence when the claim needs evidence.

## Documents

Create press kits, festival packets, cue sheets, and other requested documents.

Create a same-name Markdown companion for every rich document.
