# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate another contributor.

Review ideas and code on their technical and creative merits. Explain disagreements with evidence when possible.

Protect private user data, credentials, unpublished media, and private project information.

Repository maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.
